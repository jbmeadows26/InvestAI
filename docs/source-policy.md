# Source and forecast policy

Official Treasury source: https://home.treasury.gov/treasury-daily-interest-rate-xml-feed. Forecasts require usage rights, independent evidence, point-in-time validation, and prospective calibration; benchmark passes are research candidates only. Archive source hashes, timestamps and URLs. Holiday-aware freshness and historical revisions remain open. FRED/ALFRED, Stooq and CoinGecko excluded from training until rights are established. No personal finance data. BLS, SEC, BEA, EIA, FDIC and CFTC adapters pending.
